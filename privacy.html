Privacy Policy — Personal Drive MCP

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Personal Drive MCP is a single-user, personal-use tool. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

The tool accesses the operator's own Google Drive files and file metadata through the Google Drive API, only after the operator signs in with their own Google account and grants consent. It does not access any other person's data.

How data is used

Files and metadata are accessed only to carry out the operator's own requests, such as organizing folders, filing documents, and retrieving files. Data is processed by the operator's AI assistant solely to perform those requested tasks.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored on the operator's own computer. File contents retrieved through the tool are stored only in the operator's own accounts and devices.

Sharing

Google user data obtained through this tool is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles. No human other than the operator views the data.

Revoking access

The operator can revoke the tool's access at any time from the third-party access page of their Google Account, or by deleting the stored authorization tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the operator through the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
